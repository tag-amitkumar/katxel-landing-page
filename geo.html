How we work

Predictable from quote to handover

Recce & plan

Site reconnaissance, accuracy specification, control network design, and flight or traverse planning agreed in writing before mobilisation.

Capture

Field crews with redundant equipment and logged observations. Daily data backup, so a bad weather window costs a day and not a campaign.

Process & QC

Adjustment, residual reporting, and independent check points. You get the accuracy statement alongside the data, not on request.

Deliver

Open formats, documented CRS and metadata, and a walkthrough with whoever will use the layers day to day.

Stack
