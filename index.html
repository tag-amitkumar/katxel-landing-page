How we engage

Four steps, no surprises

The same shape whether it is a survey campaign, an HRMS rollout, a national flood model, or a training cohort.

Step one

Scope

A short discovery to pin down the actual question, the data you already hold, and what "done" looks like.

Step two

Pilot

A bounded first slice — one district, one department, one peril — so you see real output before scaling.

Step three

Build

Delivery in reviewable increments. You see working output throughout, not a single reveal at the end.

Step four

Handover

Documentation, training, and source or data handover. Ongoing support if you want it, not because you are stuck.
