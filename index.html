How we engage

Four steps, no surprises

The same shape whether it's a survey campaign, an HRMS rollout, or a national flood model.

Scope

A short paid or free discovery to pin down the actual question, the data you already hold, and what "done" looks like.

Pilot

A bounded first slice — one district, one department, one peril — so you see real output before committing to scale.

Build

Delivery in reviewable increments. You see working output throughout, not a single reveal at the end.

Handover

Documentation, training, and source or data handover. Ongoing support if you want it, not because you're stuck.
